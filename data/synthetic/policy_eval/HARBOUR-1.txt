Harbour Home Plan - Wording
PART 1 DEFINITIONS
1.1 Fixed installation
Fixed installation means the pipes, tanks and heating equipment that are permanently
connected to the main water supply of the home.
1.2 Frozen pipe
Frozen pipe means a pipe in which water has turned to ice and caused it to split.
PART 2 WHAT WE COVER
2.1 Water damage
We cover damage to the home caused by water that escapes suddenly from a fixed
installation.
2.2 Frozen pipes
We also cover the cost of repairing a frozen pipe that has split and the damage that
follows.
PART 3 WHAT WE DO NOT COVER
3.1 Neglect
We do not cover damage where you failed to maintain the pipes.
3.2 Gradual damage
We do not cover damage that happens gradually, including:
(a) rust and corrosion;
(b) a slow drip that is left unrepaired.
PART 4 SUM INSURED AND EXCESS
4.1 Sum insured
The most we will pay for all claims in one year is the sum insured shown in the Schedule.
4.2 Excess
You must pay the excess shown in the Schedule towards each claim.
PART 5 GENERAL CONDITIONS
5.1 Claims procedure
Contact us promptly after a loss and send the documents we ask for.
