Beech Residential Cover Wording
SECTION 1 WORDS EXPLAINED
1.1 Hose
Hose means a flexible pipe fitted to a washing machine or sink.
SECTION 2 WHAT WE LOOK AFTER
2.1 Failed hoses
Repair costs after a flood from a failed hose are paid by us.
2.2 Burst pipes
Escape of water from a burst pipe is something we protect you against.
SECTION 3 OUTSIDE THIS COVER
3.1 Deliberate harm
Loss from deliberate damage by you is not paid.
3.2 Mould
Mould that grows over time is outside this cover.
SECTION 4 THE NUMBERS
4.1 Twelve-month maximum
In any twelve months we pay no more than the Sum Insured.
4.2 Baths and basins
For leaks from baths and basins the ceiling is INR 40,000 each claim.
4.3 Starting contribution
Each claim starts with you paying INR 3,000.
SECTION 5 LOOKING AFTER THE HOME
5.1 Empty home
Switch off the water supply if you leave the home empty for a week or more.
5.2 Alarm
Cover only applies while the alarm is switched on at night.
SECTION 6 THE SMALL PRINT
6.1 Recovery from others
Once we have paid a claim we may take over your right to recover the money from anyone responsible.
6.2 Post
Any notice we send to you will be sent to the address in the Schedule.
6.3 Joint holders
If more than one person is named, each of you is bound by these terms.
6.4 Rounding
Payments are rounded to the nearest rupee.
6.5 Outsiders
Nobody except you and us may enforce this policy.
6.6 Plain words
We have tried to write these terms in plain words.
