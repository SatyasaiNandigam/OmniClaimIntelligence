Elmwood Household Terms
PART 1 WORDS WE USE
1.1 Pipework
Pipework means all fixed pipes, valves and tanks supplying or draining water in the home.
1.2 Home
Home means the house at the address in the Schedule and its garage.
PART 2 PROTECTION PROVIDED
2.1 Burst or overflowing water
We will repair damage caused by pipework bursting or overflowing.
2.2 Storm
We will repair damage to the home caused by storm.
PART 3 WHEN WE WILL NOT HELP
3.1 Slow deterioration
We will not repair damage resulting from rot, corrosion or long-term leaking.
3.2 Empty homes
We will not repair damage if the home was left empty for more than 45 days.
PART 4 MONEY MATTERS
4.1 Highest payment
The highest amount we will pay for any one claim is the Sum Insured in the Schedule.
4.2 Your share
You bear the first part of every claim, as shown in the Schedule.
4.3 Water ceiling
We will pay at most INR 150,000 for damage from pipework in a single claim.
PART 5 YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Telling us
Tell us within 14 days of finding any damage.
5.2 Protecting the home
Take sensible steps to stop damage getting worse.
PART 6 OTHER TERMS
6.1 Governing law
This policy is governed by the law of the country shown in the Schedule.
6.2 Dishonest claims
If a claim is dishonest we may refuse it and cancel the policy.
