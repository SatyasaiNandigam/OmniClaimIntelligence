Walnut Home Cover Wording
SECTION 1 TERMS USED
1.1 Tank
Tank means any container that stores water for the home.
SECTION 2 WHAT IS INCLUDED
2.1 Burst tanks
We will cover the repair of damage to the home from a burst water tank.
2.2 Lightning
Damage from lightning is included in this policy.
SECTION 3 WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED
3.1 War risks
Damage caused by war or nuclear activity is not included.
3.2 Ageing
No payment will be made for damage from wear and tear.
SECTION 4 PAYMENT AMOUNTS
4.1 Annual ceiling
Our total payment in any policy year will never go beyond the Sum Insured in the Schedule.
4.2 Tank cap
Damage from water tanks is capped at INR 60,000 per claim.
4.3 Your first part
The first INR 5,000 of every claim is borne by you.
SECTION 5 YOUR PART
5.1 Reporting a loss
You need to let us know about any loss within 28 days.
5.2 Living in the home
The home must be lived in for at least 180 days each year.
SECTION 6 ADMINISTRATION
6.1 Cooling off
You may cancel within 14 days of buying and receive a full refund of the premium.
6.2 Disputes
Any dispute will be decided by the courts of the country named in the Schedule.
6.3 Personal data
We use your personal information as explained in our privacy notice.
6.4 Renewal
We may refuse to renew this policy by writing to you before the end of the period.
6.5 Headings
Headings are for convenience and do not affect the meaning of these terms.
6.6 Language
This policy is written in English.
