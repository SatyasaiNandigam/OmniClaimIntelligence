Willow Home Policy Terms
SECTION A TERMS EXPLAINED
A1 Flood
Flood means water that rises from a river, lake or sea and covers normally dry land.
SECTION B WHAT YOU ARE PROTECTED AGAINST
B1 Sudden pipe failure
We will meet the cost of repairing damage caused by a pipe suddenly splitting.
B2 Fire and smoke
We will meet the cost of repairing damage caused by fire or smoke.
SECTION C WHAT IS LEFT OUT
C1 Faulty workmanship
We will not meet the cost of repairing damage caused by poor building work.
SECTION D HOW MUCH WE PAY
D1 Top amount for the policy
The top amount we will pay for all claims in a year is shown as the Sum Insured in the Schedule.
D2 Pipe damage cap
The most we will pay for repairing pipes and fittings is INR 80,000 for any one claim.
D3 First amount you pay
You are responsible for the first INR 7,500 of each claim.
SECTION E WHAT WE ASK OF YOU
E1 Keeping us informed
Contact us within 21 days of discovering a loss.
E2 Maintenance
Keep the plumbing in good working order.
SECTION F GENERAL MATTERS
F1 Ending the policy
Either of us may end this policy by giving 30 days written notice.
F2 Fraud
If you or anyone acting for you makes a fraudulent claim we will not pay it.
F3 Complaints
If you are unhappy with our service you can complain to our customer team.
F4 Paying the premium
You must pay the premium by the due date in the Schedule.
F5 Changes to these terms
We may change these terms only by writing to you at least 30 days before the change.
F6 Currency
All amounts under this policy are in Indian rupees.
