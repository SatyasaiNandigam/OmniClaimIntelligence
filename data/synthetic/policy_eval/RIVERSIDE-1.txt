Riverside Home Cover - Terms and Conditions
Article 1 - Definitions
Clause 1.1 Dwelling
The dwelling means the main house named in the Schedule.
Clause 1.2 Burst
Burst means a sudden rupture of a pipe, tank or hose.
Article 2 - Cover
Clause 2.1 Water escape
We pay for damage to the dwelling caused by a burst of a fixed pipe or tank.
Article 3 - Exclusions
Clause 3.1 Long-term seepage
We do not pay for damage from seepage that continues over weeks or months.
Clause 3.2 Unoccupied homes
We do not pay for water damage if the dwelling was empty for over 60 days.
Article 4 - Financial terms
Clause 4.1 Maximum payable
We will pay no more than the Sum Insured shown in the Schedule for any claim.
Clause 4.2 Excess
You pay the excess shown in the Schedule on every claim.
Article 5 - Your duties
Clause 5.1 Prompt notice
Tell us about a loss within 30 days.
Clause 5.2 Reasonable care
You must keep pipes and tanks in good repair and take reasonable steps to stop further damage.
